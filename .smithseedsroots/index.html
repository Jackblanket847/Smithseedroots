Our Story

Growing Gardens, Cultivating Stories

At Smith Seeds, Roots, Gardening!, we believe every seed holds a story waiting to unfold. Since 1952, we've been helping gardeners of all skill levels bring their green dreams to life.

Our collection of over 500 products spans from tiny sprouting seeds to magnificent fruit trees.

From first sprout at 3 days to harvest at 6 months, we guide you every step of the way.
